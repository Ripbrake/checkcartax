Check van tax

For many people a van is a tool of the trade. Keeping its tax up to date avoids unnecessary costs and keeps your business on the road.

UK

How van tax is calculated

Unlike cars, most vans (light goods vehicles up to 3,500kg) don't pay by CO2 emissions. Vans registered on or after 1 March 2001 pay a flat rate, with lower rates for certain Euro 4 and Euro 5 vans. Vans registered before 1 March 2001 pay according to engine size.

Light goods vehicles (vans) up to 3,500kg
Vehicle12 months6 months
Registered on or after 1 March 2001 (TC39), including zero emission£360£198
Euro 4 vans registered 1 March 2003 to 31 December 2006 (TC36)£140£77
Euro 5 vans registered 1 January 2009 to 31 December 2010 (TC36)£140£77
Registered before 1 March 2001, not over 1,549cc£230£126.50
Registered before 1 March 2001, over 1,549cc£375£206.25

Electric vans

From 1 April 2025, electric vans pay vehicle tax; GOV.UK lists zero emission vans in the same TC39 rate as other light goods vehicles registered from 1 March 2001.

Heavier vehicles

Vehicles over 3,500kg revenue weight are taxed under different rules (heavy goods vehicle rates, which can include the HGV levy). Check GOV.UK for your vehicle's class.

Tips for van owners

  • Check tax status when buying a used van; tax doesn't transfer to you.
  • If a van is laid up, make a SORN to get a refund of full months remaining.
  • Pay yearly to avoid the 5% surcharge on monthly or 6-monthly Direct Debits.

Source: GOV.UK vehicle tax rate tables. Rates change each April, so always confirm on GOV.UK before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

How much is van tax?

For most vans up to 3,500kg registered from 1 March 2001 it's £360 a year (TC39). Eligible Euro 4 and Euro 5 vans pay £140.

Do electric vans pay tax?

Yes, from 1 April 2025.
