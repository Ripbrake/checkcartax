Car tax price check

There's no single price for car tax. The amount depends on when the car was first registered, its CO2 emissions or engine size, and sometimes its list price.

UK

Step 1: find the first registration date

This is on the V5C logbook. It decides which of the three rate systems applies to you.

Step 2: find the right figure

  • Before 1 March 2001: you need the engine size in cc.
  • 1 March 2001 to 31 March 2017: you need the CO2 figure in g/km.
  • From 1 April 2017: after the first year, most cars pay the standard rate. You need to know whether the list price was over £40,000.

Step 4: choose how to pay

Paying 12 months in one go is cheapest. Monthly or 6-monthly Direct Debits add a 5% surcharge; for a standard-rate car that means £210 over 12 months instead of £200.

Tip: list price is not what you paid

The £40,000 test uses the car's published list price before its first registration, before any discounts, including options. A car you buy used for much less can still carry the supplement until 5 years after its second tax payment. Ask the dealer or seller if you're unsure.

Source: GOV.UK vehicle tax rate tables. Rates change each April, so always confirm on GOV.UK before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my car's CO2 figure?

It's printed on the V5C logbook. GOV.UK also offers an online lookup of emissions details.

Is it cheaper to pay car tax yearly?

Yes. Paying 12 months at once, by card or Direct Debit, has no surcharge. Monthly and 6-monthly Direct Debits add 5%.
