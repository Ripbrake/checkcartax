Check car road tax

The internet has made car admin easier: you can tax a vehicle online and check the road tax status of any car, including one you're thinking of buying.

UK

What a road tax check shows

A road tax check tells you whether a vehicle is currently taxed, untaxed or registered as off the road (SORN), and when the current tax expires. You only need the registration number.

How road tax is calculated

A common myth is that road tax was based on engine size until 2017. In fact, cars first registered from 1 March 2001 already paid by CO2 emissions; only cars registered before then are taxed on engine size. In April 2017 the system changed again for new cars, introducing a CO2-based first-year rate and a flat standard rate afterwards (£200 a year from 1 April 2026).

The cleanest older cars in bands A and B pay £20 a year, while the biggest emitters registered between 2001 and 2017 pay up to £790. Since April 2025, electric cars pay vehicle tax as well.

If the car is untaxed

  • If it's yours, tax it on GOV.UK, by phone on 0300 123 4321 or at a Post Office that deals with vehicle tax.
  • If it's off the road, make sure a SORN is in place.
  • If you're buying it, remember tax never transfers: you'll need to tax it in your name before driving it away.

Before you tax

The vehicle needs a valid MOT (if it requires one) when the tax starts, and you need a reference number from a V11 reminder, the V5C logbook in your name, or the green new keeper slip.

Source: GOV.UK vehicle tax rate tables. Rates change each April, so always confirm on GOV.UK before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need to tax a car?

A reference number from a recent V11 reminder, the V5C logbook in your name, or the green new keeper slip if you've just bought the car.

Can I tax a car without an MOT?

No, if the vehicle needs an MOT it must be valid when the tax starts.
